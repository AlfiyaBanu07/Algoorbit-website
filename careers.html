FAQ

Questions We Get Asked

connect@algoorbit.com 48-hour reply
Is this a paid internship?

Yes, internships at Algo Orbit are paid. The stipend varies by role and is discussed during the final conversation round. We believe your time and skills deserve fair compensation.

Can I do this remotely?

Most roles support hybrid or fully remote arrangements. For video editing and digital marketing, remote is standard. For full stack, it depends on the project. We'll clarify during the first call.

What's the duration of the internship?

Typically 2–6 months with the option to extend. High performers are considered for full-time Pre-Placement Offers (PPO) at the end of their internship.

Do I need a portfolio or GitHub to apply?

Highly recommended but not mandatory. A GitHub profile for developers or a video reel for editors significantly improves your shortlist chances. Even personal projects count — it shows initiative.

How do I apply?

Click any "Apply Now" button on this page, fill out the form, attach your resume, and tell us why you're excited about Algo Orbit. We respond within 48 hours.
