Our Legacy

A Tradition of Taste & Comfort

Nestled in the heart of Jaipur, Annapoorna Grand stands as a testament to India's rich culinary heritage and the art of gracious living. Founded in 1994 by the Sharma family, our establishment was born from a singular vision — to create a space where every guest is treated as family.

Our kitchens are commanded by master chefs who have spent decades perfecting the nuances of Rajasthani, Mughlai, and Pan-Indian cuisine, using recipes passed down through generations combined with contemporary culinary artistry.

Beyond our acclaimed dining, Annapoorna Grand offers meticulously appointed rooms and suites that marry traditional Rajput design with modern comforts, ensuring that every stay is an experience to remember.
