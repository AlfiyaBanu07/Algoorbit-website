Our Story

Where Every Stitch
Tells a Story

Bramhari Fashion Designer & Tailoring has been the most trusted name in Karkala and the surrounding Udupi district for over 14 years. Founded with a deep passion for Indian textiles and impeccable craftsmanship, we have dressed brides, grooms, families, and professionals for their most important occasions.

Located in the heart of Karkala at Fortune Lotus, our studio is a place where your vision meets our expertise. Whether you walk in with a reference image or just a feeling, we craft garments that feel like they were always meant for you.

Why Choose Bramhari

The Bramhari
Promise

We are not just a tailoring shop. We are your personal style partners — people who listen, understand, and create garments that make you feel proud every time you wear them.

01
Perfect Fit, Every Time

We take detailed measurements and conduct multiple trials to ensure your garment fits your body — not the other way around. No guesswork. Only precision.

02
Premium Fabrics from Trusted Sources

We use only the finest silks, cottons, georgettes, and brocades sourced from leading fabric markets across Karnataka and India.

03
Delivered On Time — Always

We know your occasions are fixed. Whether it is a wedding, festival, or function — your garment will be ready when we promise, without compromise on quality.

04
Personal Attention to Every Client

From your first visit to your final fitting, you receive the full attention of our designer. We remember your preferences, your style, and your family's needs.

How It Works

From Idea to Your Wardrobe

Four simple steps stand between you and the garment of your dreams.

STEP 1
Visit Us

Walk into our studio at Fortune Lotus, Karkala or call us on 7676440681 to schedule a visit at your convenience.

STEP 2
Design & Choose

Browse our reference designs, share your inspiration, choose your fabric and embellishments. We guide you at every step.

STEP 3
Stitching & Fitting

Our skilled tailors craft your garment with care. We call you for a trial fitting to ensure the perfect drape and fit before final stitching.

STEP 4
Collect & Celebrate

Your garment is pressed, packed, and ready for you. Wear it with pride — and return to us for your next occasion.
