Who We Are

Rooted in Community.
Driven by Purpose.

Founded in 2012 in Mysuru, Karnataka, HopeForward Foundation began as a small literacy initiative for out-of-school children. Over twelve years, we have grown into a multi-program organization working across education, water, women's empowerment, and environmental sustainability — serving 48,000+ lives in 8 districts of South India.

01
Community-First Approach
Every program is designed with the community, not for them. We listen before we act, and adapt continuously based on local needs and feedback.
02
Radical Transparency
We publish detailed annual reports, quarterly impact statements, and real-time fund utilization data — because trust is earned, not assumed.
03
Sustainable, Long-Term Impact
We do not believe in one-time interventions. Our programs build institutional capacity and community ownership that sustains progress for decades.

What We Run

Flagship Programs

01
Paathshala – Digital Learning Centres
Solar-powered tablet learning labs installed in government primary schools. Each centre supports 80–120 students with curriculum-aligned digital content in Kannada and Telugu, reducing dropout rates by 34% in pilot schools.
62 Centres Active
34% Dropout Reduction
02
Arogya – Mobile Health Clinics
Six mobile clinics making monthly visits to remote villages, providing free primary healthcare, maternal health services, and vaccination drives. In 2023 alone, we conducted 18,400 consultations across 90 villages.
6 Mobile Clinics
18.4K Consultations / Year
03
Vruksha – Reforestation Initiative
Community-led reforestation of degraded land around the Western Ghats buffer zone. Over 80,000 native saplings have been planted since 2018, with a 78% survival rate — restoring biodiversity and carbon sinks simultaneously.
80,000+ Saplings Planted
78% Survival Rate
04
Udyoga – Livelihood Development
Six-month vocational training programs in tailoring, electronics repair, food processing, and digital services — linked with microfinance access and market linkages. 420 graduates have established their own micro-enterprises.
2,800+ Trained
420 Enterprises Started
