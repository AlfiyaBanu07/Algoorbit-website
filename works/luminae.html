About Luminae

Designing Spaces That Inspire

Luminae is a globally recognized interior architecture studio founded on the belief that exceptional spaces have the power to transform how people live, work, and feel. Our multidisciplinary team brings together architects, designers, and artisans united by an unwavering pursuit of perfection.

From intimate private residences to landmark hospitality projects across six continents, we approach every commission with fresh eyes, deep expertise, and an uncompromising commitment to craftsmanship.

How We Work

Our Design Process

01
Discovery
We begin with deep conversations — listening to understand your vision, lifestyle, and the story you want your space to tell the world.
02
Concept Design
Translating insight into ideas — mood boards, spatial concepts, material palettes, and three-dimensional visualizations that bring the vision to life.
03
Development
Every detail is meticulously refined. Technical drawings, material sourcing, custom fabrication, and contractor coordination all managed with precision.
04
Delivery
Your space is unveiled — fully realized, perfectly finished, and ready to begin its story. We remain available for any refinements that follow.
