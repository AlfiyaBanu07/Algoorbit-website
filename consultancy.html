FAQ

Questions? We Have Answers.

Quick answers connect@algoorbit.in
How much does a project cost?

Starter projects begin from ₹1,200. MVP or web apps from ₹2,800. Enterprise platforms from ₹4,999. All final quotes are fixed price with no surprises and you will receive a detailed breakdown in your proposal within 48 hours.

How long does development take?

Simple websites take 1 to 2 weeks. MVPs and apps take 6 to 10 weeks. Enterprise platforms take 3 to 6 months. We always provide a milestone timeline upfront, and we hit them.

We are an early-stage startup. Can you work with us?

Absolutely. We love working with founders. Startup-friendly pricing, flexible payment plans, and we can act as your technical co-founder. Many of our best projects started with just an idea.

Do I own the code after the project?

100% yes. Full source code, documentation, and IP ownership transfers to you on final payment. No vendor lock-in, ever.

What about maintenance after launch?

We offer flexible monthly retainer plans covering hosting, security updates, bug fixes, and feature additions. Think of us as your ongoing tech team, not a one-off vendor.
