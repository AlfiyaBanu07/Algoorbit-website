How much does a project cost?

Starter projects begin from $1,200. MVP or web apps from $2,800. Enterprise platforms from $4,999. All final quotes are fixed price with a detailed breakdown within 48 hours.

How long does development take?

Simple websites take 1 to 2 weeks. MVPs and apps take 6 to 10 weeks. Enterprise platforms take 3 to 6 months. We always provide a milestone timeline upfront.

We are an early-stage startup. Can you work with us?

Absolutely. We offer startup-friendly pricing, flexible payment plans, and can act as your technical co-founder.

Do I own the code after the project?

100% yes. Full source code, documentation, and IP ownership transfers to you on final payment. No vendor lock-in, ever.

We offer flexible monthly retainer plans covering hosting, security updates, bug fixes, and feature additions.
