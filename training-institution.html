FAQ

Questions from institutions

Quick answers
Can programs be customised?

Yes. Each proposal is planned around your department, semester, skill levels, syllabus, timetable and budget.

What batch sizes do you support?

Batch sizes depend on the program and your lab capacity. Share your numbers and we will plan sessions to match.

Do you deliver on campus?

We can plan on campus delivery, depending on your lab availability and delivery mode. Other formats can be discussed.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on scope, duration, batch size and deliverables, and are shared in your written proposal.

Are certificates provided?

Completion certificates are provided where applicable, as agreed for each program.

What reporting do we receive, and how fast is a proposal?

You receive attendance and assessment records plus a program completion report. To get a proposal, contact us with your requirements; we will confirm the timeline when we reply.
