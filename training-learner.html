FAQ

Questions from learners

Quick answers
Can beginners join?

It depends on the program. Each one lists its eligibility and prerequisites, so you can see whether it suits your current level before enrolling.

Will I get a certificate?

Certificates are issued when you meet the completion requirements stated on the program page, such as attendance and practical work.

Will I work on projects?

Yes. Practical work is part of our programs. The specific projects and assessments are listed for each program.

Who can apply for internships?

Internship eligibility is listed on the program details. Contact us if you are unsure whether you qualify.

What are the fees?

Fees and applicable taxes are shown on each program page before you enroll.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. We do not guarantee employment. Where a program includes placement assistance, it is stated on the program page and means support with preparation and opportunities, not a promised job.
