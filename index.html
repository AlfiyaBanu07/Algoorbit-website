Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Algo Orbit.

What industries do you serve?

We serve healthcare, finance, retail, manufacturing, education, and government. Solutions are customized for each industry's requirements and compliance standards.

Do you build fully custom software?

Absolutely. Custom software is our core. From web apps and mobile apps to complex enterprise systems, we design, build, and scale solutions for your exact requirements.

Do you offer training for college students?

Yes! We run internships, final-year project guidance, workshops, and placement-focused bootcamps. We've trained 2,500+ students with a consistent 5/5 rating.

What is your typical project timeline?

Small projects typically take 2–3 months; enterprise solutions 6–12 months. We provide detailed milestones during the initial discovery phase.

What post-launch support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive post-launch support including maintenance, performance monitoring, feature updates, and dedicated technical assistance, ensuring your systems evolve with your business.
