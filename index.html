Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Algo Orbit.

What industries do you serve?

We serve healthcare, finance, retail, manufacturing, education, and government. Solutions are customized for each industry’s requirements and compliance standards.

Do you build fully custom software?

Absolutely. Custom software is our core. From web apps and mobile apps to complex enterprise systems, we design, build, and scale solutions for your exact requirements.

Do you offer training for college students?

Yes. We run internships, final year project help, workshops, and bootcamps focused on placements for colleges and their students.

What is your typical project timeline?

Small projects typically take 2 to 3 months; larger solutions 6 to 12 months. We provide detailed milestones during the initial discovery phase.

What support do you give after launch?

We keep supporting you after launch with maintenance, monitoring, new features and technical help, so your system grows with your business.
